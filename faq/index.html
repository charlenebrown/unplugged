Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before experiencing UNPLUGGED™.

Who are UNPLUGGED™ retreats designed for?

UNPLUGGED™ retreats are designed for executives, founders, entrepreneurs, and organizational leadership teams seeking space to step away from daily demands, gain fresh perspectives, and reconnect with their purpose. Whether you're navigating organizational change, strengthening team relationships, or investing in personal leadership development, our experiences combine strategic reflection, wellness, and meaningful connection.

Can organizations book private leadership retreats?

Yes. UNPLUGGED™ offers private retreat experiences designed around the unique goals and needs of leadership teams. From strengthening communication and organizational alignment to navigating transitions and building trust, each experience brings together thoughtful facilitation, restorative activities, and opportunities for meaningful collaboration.

Where do UNPLUGGED™ retreats take place?

Our retreats are envisioned in thoughtfully selected destinations around the world, including mountain sanctuaries, coastal hideaways, countryside estates, and eco-luxury resorts. Each location is chosen to provide comfort, privacy, natural beauty, and an environment that encourages reflection, connection, and renewed perspective.

Are participants required to disconnect from technology?

Not at all. At UNPLUGGED™, disconnecting is about creating intentional space, not surrendering personal autonomy. While certain activities may encourage device-free participation, these experiences are optional. Participants maintain access to their devices, and emergency communication procedures are considered throughout the retreat experience.

Can dietary and accessibility needs be accommodated?

Inclusive hospitality is an essential part of the UNPLUGGED™ experience. Our retreat planning approach considers dietary preferences, food allergies, mobility requirements, accessibility needs, and individual comfort levels. We aim to create welcoming environments where participants can engage meaningfully without unnecessary barriers. Specific accommodations would be discussed during the planning process.

How do I inquire about a retreat or partnership?

For retreat inquiries, organizational experiences, or potential collaborations, visit our About page for contact information. Whether you're exploring a private leadership retreat, a curated executive experience, or a strategic partnership, our concept is designed around thoughtful conversations that clarify your goals before planning begins.

Portfolio note: UNPLUGGED™ is a fictional brand created for demonstration purposes. These answers describe the proposed retreat experience, not currently available services or confirmed accommodations.
